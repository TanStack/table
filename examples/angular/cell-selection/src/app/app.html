Click and drag to select a range of cells. Hold Shift while clicking to extend the selection, or Ctrl/Cmd to add or subtract a rectangle. Arrow keys move the selection, Shift+Arrow extends it, Mod+A selects all, Mod+C copies, and Escape clears. Uncomment `enableCellSelection: false` on a column def to opt that column out of selection.

Hiding, reordering, and pinning columns all keep a live selection anchored to the same cell ids. Ranges are indexed in render order, so a pinned column moves the rectangle with it rather than splitting it.
